Care Coordination Organizations (CCOs) serving all 5 NYC boroughs and how to access OPWDD services through the Front Door process.

NYC Care Coordination Organizations (CCOs)

These three organizations provide Medicaid Service Coordination (MSC) and Health Home care management for people with intellectual/developmental disabilities (I/DD) enrolled in OPWDD services. All three serve all 5 NYC boroughs.

Care Coordination Organization
Care Design NY

Provides comprehensive Health Home care management and Medicaid Service Coordination for people with I/DD enrolled in OPWDD services across New York, including all 5 NYC boroughs.

📞 Call (518) 235-1888 🌐 caredesignny.org

Tri-County Care

A New York CCO providing Health Home care management and Medicaid Service Coordination for people with I/DD enrolled in OPWDD services, with offices in Brooklyn, Long Island City, and other locations.

The OPWDD “Front Door” Process

The Front Door is the standard entry process for accessing OPWDD services in New York State. Here is how it works, step by step:

Step 1
Contact the Front Door or a Care Manager

Call the OPWDD Infoline or contact your local OPWDD Regional Office to get started. You can also reach out directly to a Care Coordination Organization (see above). Front Door staff will guide you through the process.

📞 OPWDD Infoline: 1-866-946-9733 🌐 opwdd.ny.gov/get-started/front-door
📞 Call 1-866-946-9733 🌐 OPWDD Front Door
Step 2
Complete Eligibility Determination

OPWDD eligibility is based on documentation of an intellectual or developmental disability (I/DD) that began before age 22. You will need to provide diagnostic records, psychological evaluations, or other documentation. OPWDD staff review your records and make an eligibility determination.

Step 3
Enroll in a Care Coordination Organization

Once found eligible, you will enroll with one of the Care Coordination Organizations (CCOs) listed above. Your CCO assigns you a Care Manager who coordinates your services and supports.

Step 4
Develop a Life Plan

Together with your Care Manager, family, and other people you choose, you develop a Life Plan — a person-centered plan that describes your goals, strengths, needs, and the services and supports that will help you live the life you want.

Step 5
Access Services Through Your Life Plan

With your Life Plan in place, your Care Manager helps you access OPWDD-funded services such as residential supports, day programs, respite, employment services, community habilitation, and more. Services are authorized and coordinated through your plan.

State Agency
OPWDD – Office for People With Developmental Disabilities

New York State agency that coordinates services and supports for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities. Contact them to start the Front Door process, find a regional office, or learn about available programs.
