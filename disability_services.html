Eligibility & Entry Process
OPWDD Front Door

The Front Door is OPWDD’s person-centered process for establishing eligibility and access to services. Follow these steps to get started:

1. Call the OPWDD Infoline (1-866-946-9733) and ask to be transferred to your local Front Door office by county.
2. Attend a Front Door information session to learn about eligibility and available services.
3. Submit a complete eligibility packet: medical, psychological, and educational evaluations documenting a qualifying developmental disability (autism, intellectual disability, Down syndrome, etc.), plus 3+ adaptive scores in the low range.
4. Complete a DDP-2 phone interview, usually about one week after the Front Door session.
5. Receive an eligibility determination letter, typically within 2–4 weeks.
6. Once eligible, choose a Care Coordination Organization (CCO) and enroll with a Care Manager to build a person-centered Life Plan. The full process usually takes 3–6 months.

Care Coordination Organization (CCO)
Care Design NY

One of the three Care Coordination Organizations (CCOs) serving all five NYC boroughs. Provides Health Home Care Management, combining developmental disability services with health and wellness coordination for people with I/DD.

📍 Serves all 5 boroughs; borough enrollment specialists available (Bronx, Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan, Staten Island) 📞 518-235-1888
📞 Call 518-235-1888 🌐 caredesignny.org

Tri-County Care

One of the three Care Coordination Organizations (CCOs) serving all five NYC boroughs, including Brooklyn, Queens, Staten Island, Bronx, and Manhattan. Provides Health Home Care Management for people with I/DD.

📍 Serves all 5 boroughs; NYC offices in Brooklyn and Long Island City 📞 1-844-504-8400
