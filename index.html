About Penny

Hey, I'm Penny. 👋 Scrappy resource wizard, senior peer specialist, and professional "wait, have you tried calling that number instead" person. I built this site because navigating healthcare, benefits, and support systems shouldn't feel like a scavenger hunt — so I did the digging, so you don't have to. If it helps even one person get the support they're owed a little faster, it did its job. 💜

How This Site Works

This site is a directory of free and low-cost support resources across New York City, covering healthcare, housing, education, technology access, and LGBTQIA2S+ specific services. Everything is organized by category in the navigation menu at the top of the page: browse there to find hotlines, clinics, benefits programs, and community organizations relevant to what you need.
