How This Site Works

This site is a directory of free and low-cost support resources across New York City, covering healthcare, housing, education, technology access, and LGBTQIA2S+ specific services. Everything is organized by category in the navigation menu at the top of the page: browse there to find hotlines, clinics, benefits programs, and community organizations relevant to what you need.
