About Penny

Hey, I'm Penny. 👋 Scrappy resource wizard, senior peer specialist, and professional "wait, have you tried calling that number instead" person. Starting an Executive Function Journey with Me

I'm now offering executive function support, one-on-one sessions to help you build systems, structure, and momentum around the stuff that feels impossible to start (or finish). This work is priced on a sliding scale: $0.50, $1.00, or $1.50 per minute, whatever fits your ability to pay. No one is turned away for not having enough, and no one is asked to prove anything. You just pick what's honest for you.

Prefer ongoing support instead of booking one session at a time? A recurring subscription option is also available for consistent, on-demand access, so you don't have to start from scratch every time something comes up.
