How This Site Works

All resource categories are available in the navigation menu at the top of the page. Use the links below as a quick guide to what each section contains:

  • LGBTQIA2S+ — Affirming healthcare, crisis support, community hotlines, and services for youth, elders, and the trans community.
  • Healthcare — Free and low-cost clinics, mental health support, primary care, crisis lines, and OPWDD / I/DD care coordination and the Front Door process.
  • Housing — Affordable housing lotteries, tenant rights support, homelessness prevention, and disability-related housing programs.
  • Education — GED prep, English language classes, career training, free library programs, and online learning opportunities.
  • Technology — Free internet access, low-cost devices, digital literacy programs, and assistance getting and staying connected.
