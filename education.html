Vocational Rehabilitation
ACCES-VR (Adult Career and Continuing Education Services – Vocational Rehabilitation)

A New York State Education Department program that helps people with disabilities achieve and maintain employment and support independent living. A Vocational Rehabilitation Counselor helps develop an individualized plan and can provide services like job training, education support, assistive technology, and job placement. Eligibility-based, not automatic entitlement.

📞 1-800-222-JOBS (5627) 🌐 acces.nysed.gov/vr

Supported Education
Blueprint Supported Education

A NYC-based program (via Community Access) providing free, one-on-one support to adults 18+ living in NYC who have a history of mental health conditions and want to pursue high school equivalency, post-secondary degrees, or vocational certification. Focuses on education as a core part of mental health recovery and wellness, includes project-based learning and college/career readiness support.

📍 New York City (via Community Access) 💰 Free for eligible adults 18+
