Adult Education
NYC Office of Adult and Continuing Education (OACE)

Part of the NYC Department of Education — offers free GED preparation, English for Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL), and career and technical training programs including electrical, carpentry, Microsoft Office certification, and web design.

🏛️ NYC Dept of Education — multiple locations citywide 💰 Free for eligible NYC adults
🌐 Learn More
Higher Education
CUNY Adult Education Programs

The City University of New York offers certificate and degree programs, as well as credit and non-credit courses across its many campuses throughout New York City. Programs span workforce development, healthcare, technology, and more.

🏛️ Multiple campuses across NYC 💰 Variable cost; financial aid available
🌐 CUNY Programs
Free Library Services
NYC Public Library Systems

New York's three public library systems — New York Public Library (Manhattan, Bronx, Staten Island), Brooklyn Public Library, and Queens Public Library — offer free GED prep, English classes, career resources, and free access to LinkedIn Learning with your library card.

💰 Free with library card 📍 Branches throughout all five boroughs
🌐 NYPL Learning 🌐 Brooklyn PL
Online Learning
Coursera & edX — Free Online Courses

Both platforms offer free-to-audit online courses from universities and institutions worldwide, covering everything from coding and data science to business, healthcare, and the arts. Certificates available for a fee; auditing content is usually free.

💰 Free to audit; certificates cost extra 🌐 Fully online — available anywhere
🌐 Coursera 🌐 edX
