Free Access
NYC Public Libraries — Free Internet & Computers

All NYC public library branches offer free in-library internet access and computer use with no library card required. Libraries also run digital literacy programs to help build basic computer and internet skills. The New York Public Library (NYPL) is a great starting point for Manhattan, the Bronx, and Staten Island.

💰 Free — no card required for computer use 📍 Branches in all five boroughs

Federal Program
Federal Lifeline Program

A federal program that provides discounted phone and internet service for low-income households. Eligible households can receive up to $9.25/month credit toward their phone or internet bill (more on Tribal lands). Apply through the National Verifier at lifelinesupport.org.

💰 Up to $9.25/month discount on phone or internet 🌐 Apply at lifelinesupport.org
🌐 Apply at lifelinesupport.org
Directory
National Digital Inclusion Alliance (NDIA)

A national nonprofit that advocates for digital equity. Their website includes a directory of low-cost internet plans and local digital inclusion programs, searchable by state and city.

🌐 digitalinclusion.org
🌐 digitalinclusion.org
Devices & Training
human-I-T

Nonprofit that connects low-income individuals and nonprofits with free or low-cost refurbished computers and other devices, subsidized internet service, and digital skills training. Focuses on equity-centered digital access.

💰 Free and low-cost refurbished devices 🌐 human-i-t.org
