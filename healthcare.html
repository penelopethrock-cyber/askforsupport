NYC Care

Low-cost/no-cost health services for New Yorkers without health insurance, via NYC Health + Hospitals. Choose your own doctor regardless of immigration status or ability to pay.

📍 Citywide, via NYC Health + Hospitals 📞 1-646-NYC-CARE (1-646-692-2273)

Free confidential health care for uninsured adults, open Saturday mornings. Run by volunteer medical professionals through the Institute for Family Health.

📍 Walton Family Health Center, 1894 Walton Avenue, Bronx, NY 10453 📞 (347) 854-0170 🕒 Saturday mornings (uninsured adults)
