City Program
NYC Care

Low- and no-cost healthcare access program for uninsured New Yorkers. Run by NYC Health + Hospitals — connects you to a primary care provider regardless of immigration status or ability to pay.

📞 1-646-NYC-CARE (646-692-2273)
