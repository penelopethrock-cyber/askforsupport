Know This Before You Apply

Why Medicaid enrollment matters: If you qualify for Medicaid, enrolling isn't just about your own coverage — utilization data is part of what states and advocates use to justify keeping (and expanding) Medicaid programs and eligibility. Every enrolled person who uses their coverage helps demonstrate real demand.

Marking "yes, I have a disability": This question on Medicaid/Marketplace applications is not asking whether you receive Social Security Disability (SSDI/SSI) — it's asking about your health and whether you have a condition that qualifies as a disability under the ADA. The ADA's definition is broad: a physical or mental impairment that substantially limits a major life activity. This includes many mental health conditions — generalized anxiety disorder, depression, PTSD, OCD, and others — when they meaningfully affect your daily functioning. Answering "yes" if this applies to you can open the door to disability-related pathways and protections, and you do not need an SSDI/SSI determination or a lawyer's letter to say yes here.
